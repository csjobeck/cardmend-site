Merge duplicates, lose nothing

Cardmend finds cards for the same person, even with a different spelling, a nickname or no name at all, and shows exactly what the merged card will keep before you tap. Every number, email, address, birthday and photo ends up on one card.

Numbers that work everywhere

Numbers saved without a country code get one, in one clean format, so they work when you travel, in WhatsApp and Signal, and on cards you share.

A backup before every change

Before any merge or fix, the cards involved are backed up to your own iCloud Drive, encrypted with a key in your iCloud Keychain. Any change can be undone, any time. Backups and restores are always free.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your contacts are read and changed only on your iPhone and never leave it, except into your own encrypted iCloud backup. Anonymous usage counts help improve the app, and you can turn them off. Privacy policy

One price, no subscription

The full scan is free and includes 25 fixes. Cardmend Pro unlocks unlimited fixes with one payment, shared with your Family Sharing group.
