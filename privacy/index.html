Cardmend

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Your contacts are read and changed only on your iPhone, and backups go to your own iCloud account. I never see any of it. The only thing Cardmend sends me is anonymous usage data, which you can turn off, and which never includes anything from your contacts.

Who is responsible

Cardmend is made by Christian Sjøbeck, Denmark. Contact: christian@sjobeckdigital.dk.

Your contacts

  • Contacts access is used only to find duplicates, tidy numbers and names, and make the changes you confirm. Your contacts are never uploaded to me or anyone else.
  • Backups of the cards a change touches are saved before every change, in Cardmend's folder in your own iCloud Drive (or on your iPhone if iCloud Drive is off). They're encrypted with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain. Delete them in the app or in the Files app.
  • Photos you add are picked by you with Apple's photo picker; Cardmend only sees the photo you choose, and finds the face on your iPhone.
  • Social profile files you import (your own LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram data download) are read and matched on your iPhone and kept only on it. "Forget" removes them.

Gravatar (optional)

If you turn on Gravatar photo lookup, Cardmend sends a one-way SHA-256 code of an email address to gravatar.com for contacts without a photo, and shows any photo the owner of that address has published there. The address itself isn't sent and can't be recovered from the code. Gravatar's own privacy policy applies to those requests. It's off until you turn it on.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your payment details.

Anonymous usage data

To see whether Cardmend is used and which features help, it sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany that processes data on my behalf.

A signal records that something happened, with coarse details. For example:

  • a scan finished, with the number of contacts and issues found in broad ranges such as "101–500"
  • duplicates were merged, numbers formatted, names fixed or cards removed, with the number in ranges
  • a backup was made, restored or exported
  • a data export from LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram was imported (which service, and the number of people in ranges)
  • the unlock screen was shown, or Cardmend Pro was bought or restored

Each signal also includes general technical details such as the app version, iOS version, device model and language. Signals never contain names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, photos or anything else from your contacts.

Instead of an identifier that could identify you, TelemetryDeck receives a hashed, anonymous identifier, so I can count how many people use the app but can't tell who they are. The data isn't linked to you, isn't used for advertising, and isn't combined with data from other apps or websites. TelemetryDeck doesn't sell or share it.

To turn it off, open Cardmend, tap Settings, then turn off Share anonymous usage. Nothing is sent after that.

Ads and tracking

Cardmend has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Cardmend is not directed at children and doesn't collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

Your contacts and backups are on your devices and in your own iCloud account; delete backups in the app or the Files app. The usage data is anonymous, so I can't find, export or delete it for a particular person. Switching off Share anonymous usage stops it immediately. Under the GDPR you can contact me with any questions, and you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes
