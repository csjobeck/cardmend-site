Cardmend

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Cardmend collects no data. Your contacts are read and changed only on your iPhone, and backups go to your own iCloud account. I never see any of it.

Who is responsible

Cardmend is made by Christian Sjøbeck, Denmark. Contact: christian@sjobeckdigital.dk.

Your contacts

  • Contacts access is used only to find duplicates, tidy numbers and names, and make the changes you confirm. Your contacts are never uploaded to me or anyone else.
  • Backups of the cards a change touches are saved before every change, in Cardmend's folder in your own iCloud Drive (or on your iPhone if iCloud Drive is off). They're encrypted with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain. Delete them in the app or in the Files app.
  • Photos you add are picked by you with Apple's photo picker; Cardmend only sees the photo you choose, and finds the face on your iPhone.
  • Social profile files you import (your own LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram data download) are read and matched on your iPhone and kept only on it. "Forget" removes them.

Gravatar (optional)

If you turn on Gravatar photo lookup, Cardmend sends a one-way SHA-256 code of an email address to gravatar.com for contacts without a photo, and shows any photo the owner of that address has published there. The address itself isn't sent and can't be recovered from the code. Gravatar's own privacy policy applies to those requests. It's off until you turn it on.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your payment details.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Cardmend has no analytics, no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Cardmend is not directed at children and doesn't collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

Because Cardmend collects nothing, there is nothing I hold about you. Under the GDPR you can contact me with any questions, and you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
