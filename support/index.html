Undo a merge or a fix

Open Backups. Every change has a backup named after it, like "Before merging 3 duplicates". Tap it and choose Restore: the cards go back to how they were, and merged cards come back. Other contacts aren't touched, and your current state is backed up first, so a restore can be undone too.

Cardmend only sees some of my contacts

You chose limited access when asked. Open Settings → Apps → Cardmend → Contacts and choose Full Access. Duplicates can only be found by looking at every card.

Where are my backups?

In the Files app → iCloud Drive → Cardmend → Backups. They're encrypted with a key in your iCloud Keychain, so they open on any of your devices signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Keychain on. Use Export as vCard in Backups for a plain copy you can open anywhere.

A merge failed

Some accounts, such as company Exchange address books, don't allow changes. Cardmend then cancels the whole change and tells you; nothing is half-done.

Why can't Cardmend fetch photos from Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn?

These networks don't let apps read your friends' photos. Cardmend links profiles from the data you download from them yourself, and you can add any photo from your library. It finds the face and crops it for you.

Restore my purchase

Open Settings in Cardmend and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing members get Cardmend Pro too.
